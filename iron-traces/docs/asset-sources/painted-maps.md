# 手绘简报地图
工具：内置 image_gen。六张原图保存于 public/images/map-{normandy,falaise,market-garden,aachen,ardennes,remagen}.png。

共同提示：square full-bleed antique WWII military briefing illustrated map, ink and watercolor on aged cream parchment, organic forests, tiny detailed roofed houses, winding roads, contour lines, red dashed route, compass, no labels, no regular grid, no blocky rectangles. Artistic schematic not historical survey.
